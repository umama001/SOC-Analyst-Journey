# 🚨 Suspicious Domain Investigation Report

## Incident Summary
A DNS request was detected for a potentially malicious domain free-gift-card123.xyz, which may indicate phishing or scam-related activity.

---

## Incident Details
- Domain: free-gift-card123.xyz
- Activity: DNS request observed
-  Incident Type: Suspicious Domain Access
- Severity: 🟠 Medium

---

## Investigation & Analysis
- The domain contains keywords like “free” and “gift card”, commonly used in phishing and scam campaigns.
- The domain uses a .xyz TLD, which is frequently abused for malicious or low-reputation sites.
- The structure of the domain appears unprofessional and randomly generated, which is uncommon for legitimate businesses.
- No known trusted brand is associated with this domain.

---

## Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)
Domain: free-gift-card123.xyz

---

## Security Assessment
- High likelihood of social engineering attack
- Potential risks include:
- Credential harvesting
- Fake promotional scams
- Malware delivery

---

## Conclusion
The domain free-gift-card123.xyz is highly suspicious and likely with phishing or scam activity. User interaction with this domain could lead to credential theft or malware infection.

---

## Recommended Actions
- Escalate to higher-level analyst for further investigation
- Block the domain at DNS/firewall level
- Check if any users accessed the domain
- Monitor for similar domain patterns
